Venue graph calibration

  1. Load data/venue_graph.json and the matching plan image from data/room-screenshots/ (level-1-1.png and so on).
  2. Pick the level. Drag the dots (nodes) and the blue room boxes onto the right spot. Edges follow the dots.
  3. Click a room or a dot to select it. Arrow keys nudge it by 1 px (Shift: 5 px). Hold Alt while dragging or pressing arrows to resize a room.
  4. Save, replace data/venue_graph.json, run the tests.
